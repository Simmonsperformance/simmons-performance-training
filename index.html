02About the coach

Meet the
coach.

Built on experience, education, and the lessons learned through hard work and recovery.

Coach & Founder — Kaleb Simmons

Exercise Science major at Ottawa University Arizona (OUAZ) and college football player at OUAZ.

My athletic experience and education have shaped the Simmons Performance Training philosophy from the ground up. I have spent years learning how the body moves, how it responds to training, and what it takes to improve at the highest level. I have also personally gone through three surgeries related to an ACL injury, which taught me firsthand how demanding the process of rehab, rebuilding strength, and restoring confidence can be.

That experience gave me a deeper understanding of the importance of proper strength development, movement preparation, progressive training, recovery, and injury-risk reduction. It also reinforced that the best athletes are not only more explosive and more powerful, but also more resilient, better prepared, and more confident in their bodies. Simmons Performance Training is built around helping athletes become stronger, faster, more explosive, more confident, and better prepared for the demands of their sport. We focus on reducing injury risk, building resilience, strengthening weaknesses, and helping athletes prepare their bodies for sport through smart, intentional work.

07FAQ

Frequently asked
questions.

What ages do you train?

We train youth athletes, middle school athletes, high school athletes, college athletes, and adult fitness clients depending on the goals and training needs.

Do you train beginners?

Yes. We work with beginners and athletes at all levels by customizing training to current ability, experience, and goals.

What sports do you train for?

We work with athletes from different sports, with sessions customized to the demands of each sport and position.

Where are sessions held?

Sessions are held in Phoenix, Arizona. The exact training location may vary based on the program and arrangement.

How long are training sessions?

Sessions are structured around the athlete's training plan and program, with an emphasis on quality movement, performance development, and progression.

What should I bring to training?

Bring athletic shoes, comfortable training clothes, any relevant gear, and anything you may need for hydration or comfort during the session.

How do I book a session?

Reach out through the contact form or email to start the conversation and discuss your goals, availability, and training options.

Do you offer individual training?

Yes. Individual training is available and tailored to the athlete's goals, schedule, and current ability level.

Do you offer small-group training?

Yes. Small-group sessions are available for athletes who want a competitive, high-energy training environment.

Do you offer digital workout programs?

Yes. Digital training options are planned for future development and can be structured around athlete goals and performance needs.

Do you offer nutrition guides?

Yes. Nutrition resources are part of the future digital offering and will be structured to support training and recovery.

Can training be customized to my goals?

Absolutely. Training is personalized based on age, experience, goals, sport, current ability, and the demands of the athlete's season.
