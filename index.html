02About the coach

Meet the
coach.

Built on experience, education, and the lessons learned through hard work and recovery.

Coach & Founder — Kaleb Simmons

Exercise Science major at Ottawa University Arizona (OUAZ) and college football player at OUAZ.

My athletic experience and education have shaped the Simmons Performance Training philosophy from the ground up. I have spent years learning how the body moves, how it responds to training, and what it takes to improve at the highest level. I have also personally gone through three surgeries related to an ACL injury, which taught me firsthand how demanding the process of rehab, rebuilding strength, and restoring confidence can be.

That experience gave me a deeper understanding of the importance of proper strength development, movement preparation, progressive training, recovery, and injury-risk reduction. It also reinforced that the best athletes are not only more explosive and more powerful, but also more resilient, better prepared, and more confident in their bodies. Simmons Performance Training is built around helping athletes become stronger, faster, more explosive, more confident, and better prepared for the demands of their sport. We focus on reducing injury risk, building resilience, strengthening weaknesses, and helping athletes prepare their bodies for sport through smart, intentional work.

07FAQ

Frequently asked
questions.

What ages do you train?

We train youth athletes, middle school athletes, high school athletes, college athletes, and adult fitness clients depending on the goals and training needs.

Do you train beginners?

Yes. We work with beginners and athletes at all levels by customizing training to current ability, experience, and goals.

What sports do you train for?

We work with athletes from different sports, with sessions customized to the demands of each sport and position.

Where are sessions held?

Sessions are held in Phoenix, Arizona. The exact training location may vary based on the program and arrangement.

How long are training sessions?

Sessions are structured around the athlete's training plan and program, with an emphasis on quality movement, performance development, and progression.

What should I bring to training?

Bring athletic shoes, comfortable training clothes, any relevant gear, and anything you may need for hydration or comfort during the session.

How do I book a session?

Choose a training option in the Programs section and use the appointment form to request a session. Your appointment is confirmed only after Simmons Performance Training confirms the date and time.

Do you offer individual training?

Yes. Individual training is available and tailored to the athlete's goals, schedule, and current ability level.

Do you offer small-group training?

Yes. Small-group sessions are available for athletes who want a competitive, high-energy training environment.

Do you offer digital workout programs?

Explore the available online training programs in the Online Program section. Choose an option that fits your goals and review its details before purchasing.

Do you offer nutrition guides?

Yes. Available digital nutrition guides are listed in the Online Program section and are designed to support nutrition, training, and recovery.

Can training be customized to my goals?

Absolutely. Training is personalized based on age, experience, goals, sport, current ability, and the demands of the athlete's season.

Policies
& guidelines.

Review these policies before booking training or purchasing a digital product. Contact Simmons Performance Training with questions.

01

Cancellation Policy

Clients should provide at least 24 hours' notice to cancel a training session using the booking system or by contacting Simmons Performance Training.

Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice and missed appointments may be treated as a used prepaid session or charged under the terms disclosed for the purchased service, subject to applicable law. Exceptions may be considered for emergencies.

If Simmons Performance Training cancels a paid session, the client will be offered a replacement time, credit, or appropriate refund for the affected session.

02

Rescheduling Policy

Please request schedule changes at least 24 hours before your appointment whenever possible. Rescheduling depends on availability.

A request is not confirmed until Simmons Performance Training confirms the new date and time. If a suitable replacement cannot be arranged for a late request, the cancellation policy may apply.

If the business needs to reschedule, you will be offered an available alternative. Do not assume a session has moved until you receive confirmation.

03

Refund Policy

Refund requests for unused prepaid training sessions or packages are reviewed individually, taking into account services already provided, the terms disclosed at purchase, and applicable law. Completed sessions generally are not refundable just because a client changes their mind.

Digital products, including PDF nutrition guides and fitness programs, are generally not refundable for change of mind after delivery or issuance of the secure download link. If a file is missing, corrupted, inaccessible, or materially different from its description, contact us so we can investigate and provide an appropriate remedy where required.

This policy does not limit any rights that cannot legally be excluded. To request a review, email simmonsperftraining@gmail.com with your name, order or appointment details, purchase date, and the issue.

04

Training Waiver & Safety

Strength training, conditioning, sprinting, jumping, agility drills, and other exercise involve inherent risks, including falls, collisions, strains, sprains, and other injuries. Clients must follow coaching and equipment instructions, communicate relevant limitations, and stop and report activity if they feel unwell or unsafe.

Training is educational and is not medical diagnosis or treatment. Clients are responsible for seeking appropriate medical advice when needed and following any medical restrictions. Results vary and are not guaranteed.

Before your first session: each participant must review and sign the separate Simmons Performance Training participation waiver and health acknowledgment. A parent or legal guardian must complete the required consent process for participants under 18. The waiver must be finalized with Arizona legal counsel before it is used. This website policy is not itself the signed waiver or a release of liability.

05

Privacy Policy

Simmons Performance Training may collect information you provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, appointment preferences, training goals, athlete intake responses, order details, payment status, and messages. For minors, parent or guardian contact and consent information may be collected when needed.

Information is used to respond to requests, manage appointments, provide training, verify purchases, deliver digital products, provide support, maintain business records, and meet legal obligations. Website hosting, database/storage, email, and payment service providers may process information as needed to provide their services.

We do not sell personal information as a routine business practice. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no electronic system can be guaranteed completely secure. Records may be retained for legitimate business, security, tax, dispute, and legal purposes. You may request access to or correction of your information where appropriate by emailing simmonsperftraining@gmail.com.

06

Terms of Service

Appointment submissions are requests, not confirmed bookings. A session is confirmed only when Simmons Performance Training confirms the date and time. Clients must provide accurate contact details, arrive prepared, communicate relevant exercise limitations, and follow reasonable safety instructions.

Payment may need to be verified before a booking or digital order is confirmed or fulfilled. After an eligible digital order is verified, the purchased PDF may be delivered by secure download link to the email address provided. Clients are responsible for providing a correct email address and keeping purchased files and download links secure.

Digital guides and programs are for the purchaser's personal use. They may not be resold, publicly shared, or redistributed without written permission, except where permitted by law. Educational content does not replace individualized medical advice and does not guarantee results.

Services, purchases, cancellations, rescheduling, and refunds are subject to these policies and applicable law. Website content, branding, and digital materials remain the property of Simmons Performance Training or their respective rights holders. Questions: simmonsperftraining@gmail.com.
